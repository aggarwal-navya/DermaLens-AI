How it works

From photo to explained result in seconds

  1. 1

    Upload a photo

    A close, well-lit photo of the affected skin — from your gallery or phone camera.

  2. 2

    Add context

    Optionally add age group, sex and body site. Clinicians use these too — so does the model.

  3. 3

    Ensemble + fusion

    The best fine-tuned vision models are combined into an ensemble and fused with your metadata.

  4. 4

    Explained result

    Top match, calibrated confidence, alternatives and a heatmap of the regions that mattered.

Features

Designed for real-world, diverse skin

Tuned on Indian skin

Six-class detection trained on DermaCon-IN and SkinDisNet, datasets of Indian and South Asian patients.

Metadata fusion

Age group, sex and body site are fused with image features, mirroring how dermatologists reason.

Explainable heatmaps

Grad-CAM overlays show which regions drove the prediction, so results can be checked, not just trusted.

Knows when it doesn't know

Open-set rejection flags conditions outside its training classes, and photos that are not skin at all.

Calibrated confidence

Temperature-scaled probabilities, so “80%” means roughly 80% — not an over-confident guess.

Privacy first

Photos are processed in memory and never stored. No account, no tracking, nothing saved on the server.

Conditions covered

Six classes, plus “not identified”

If a photo doesn't clearly match one of these, DermaLens says so instead of forcing a label.

Model performance

Held-out test set results

Evaluated on a patient-grouped test split never seen during training. Read the evaluation method.
